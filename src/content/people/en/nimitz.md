Chester William Nimitz was a United States Navy officer who commanded the Pacific Fleet during the Second World War. A submarine specialist earlier in his career, he was appointed Commander in Chief of the Pacific Fleet shortly after the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor in December 1941.

Nimitz oversaw the naval war against Japan, and forces under his direction won the pivotal Battle of Midway in June 1942, which crippled the Japanese carrier fleet and shifted the strategic balance in the Pacific. He subsequently directed the island-hopping campaigns across the central Pacific, including the long struggle for Guadalcanal and the major fleet actions at Leyte Gulf in 1944.

Promoted to the newly created rank of Fleet Admiral in December 1944, Nimitz signed the Japanese instrument of surrender on behalf of the United States aboard the USS Missouri on September 2, 1945. He later served as Chief of Naval Operations from 1945 to 1947.
