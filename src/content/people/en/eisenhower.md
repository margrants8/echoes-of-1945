Dwight D. Eisenhower — known universally as "Ike" — served as Supreme Commander of Allied Expeditionary Forces in Europe during World War II.

In 1943, Eisenhower was appointed to lead Operation Overlord, planning and commanding the D-Day landings in Normandy on June 6, 1944. His exceptional ability to coordinate military forces from different nations and branches of service demonstrated rare strategic and diplomatic skill.

On May 8, 1945, Eisenhower accepted Germany's unconditional surrender on behalf of the Allied powers. After the war, he served as the 34th President of the United States from 1953 to 1961.
