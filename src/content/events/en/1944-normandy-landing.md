On June 6, 1944, the Allied forces launched Operation Overlord — the Normandy landings — the largest amphibious assault in human history.

More than 150,000 soldiers landed on the Normandy coast of France in a single day, storming five beaches codenamed Utah, Omaha, Gold, Juno, and Sword. Omaha Beach saw the heaviest casualties: the U.S. 1st Infantry Division suffered devastating losses under withering German fire from the bluffs above.

The operation opened a second front in Western Europe, trapping Germany between Allied forces advancing from the west and Soviet forces pushing from the east. It stands as one of the war's most decisive turning points. By August 25, 1944, Paris was liberated and Operation Overlord was complete.
